What the service does

PraiseLoop imports the reviews you provide, picks quotes, writes captions, renders branded quote-card images, and puts drafts in a weekly queue for you to review on the web or in the phone app. It can send you a notification when a week's drafts are ready.

You can edit a caption, approve a post, archive it, or mark it as shared after you post it yourself through your phone's share sheet. The app records a shared post as something you reported, not as a verified publication.

Nothing is posted automatically. You decide whether and where to post. Automatic scheduling through Buffer is not available yet. Google review sync is not available yet. Features that are marked as planned are not part of the service until we make them available.

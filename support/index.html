Support

Need a hand? Email hello@praiseloop.ca. We usually reply within 2 business days, Monday to Friday, Ontario time.

What to include

So we can help faster, tell us:

  • the email address on your account;
  • your business name;
  • the page or feature you were using;
  • what you expected and what happened;
  • a screenshot or the exact error text, if you have one.

Please do not send your password, a sign-in code or an access token. We never need those.

Common questions

How does approval work?

Each draft has a caption and an image. You can edit the caption, approve the draft, or archive it. Approving does not post anything by itself: you post an approved draft yourself, or, on a paid plan, choose a time and let automatic posting send it. Nothing is posted without your approval. If something was posted that you did not approve, email us right away with your business name and a link to the post.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Paid plans are not live yet. When they are, you will cancel from your account page, and the terms will state the price, billing period and refund rules before you pay. See the terms of service.

How do I delete my account?

Open your account page, choose Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. This permanently removes your account, businesses, reviews, drafts, images, sign-in methods and sessions. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in the store. For privacy requests, email privacy@praiseloop.ca.

How do I disconnect Instagram, Facebook or Buffer?

Open Connections from the profile menu and choose Disconnect in the section for that service. This removes PraiseLoop's saved access. To remove PraiseLoop from the service itself, revoke it in Facebook's or Buffer's settings.

How do I turn off the weekly email?

On your account page, find Emails and turn off the weekly "posts are ready" email. Your drafts still appear under This week.

How do I import my reviews?

Open Reviews and upload a CSV file or a Google Takeout export. Use reviews for your own business, and only if you have the right to use them.

How do I get an invite?

PraiseLoop is invite-only during the pilot. Email hello@praiseloop.ca with your business name and the address you would like to sign in with. We will reply if there is room.

Still stuck?

Email hello@praiseloop.ca with the details above. For how we handle your data, see the privacy policy. For the service agreement, see the terms of service.

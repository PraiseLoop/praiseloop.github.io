Turn your customer reviews into social posts you approve.

PraiseLoop finds the best quotes in your reviews, writes a caption in your business's voice, and makes a branded image for you to review.

Ask about the pilot

PraiseLoop is in a pilot with a small number of local businesses.

How it works

A weekly loop, with you at the decision point.

  1. 1

    We find the best quotes

    You import your customer reviews from a CSV file. PraiseLoop ranks the strongest quotes and keeps the customer's original words.

  2. 2

    We write the caption and make the image

    Each draft gets a caption in your business's voice and a branded quote card using your logo, colours and fonts.

  3. 3

    You approve and share

    Your drafts appear in the PraiseLoop app on your phone. Review each one, then share the posts you approve to Instagram or Facebook through your phone's share sheet.

Nothing is posted automatically. Every post waits for your approval.

You stay in control

  • Nothing is posted without your approval. PraiseLoop never posts on its own.
  • You can edit the caption, ask for a new version, or discard any draft before it goes out.
  • Quotes come from your own customer reviews. We do not write or invent reviews.
  • Deleting your account removes your data. The privacy policy explains what is removed.

Who it is for

PraiseLoop is built for local businesses with plenty of reviews and little time to write posts. It suits the owner or manager who wants consistent posting without hiring an agency.

Questions

Does it post without me?

No. Every post waits for your approval, and PraiseLoop does not post on its own. You share approved posts from your phone.

Which networks can I post to?

Instagram and Facebook, through your phone's share sheet today. Optional scheduling through Buffer is being added; it is not available yet.

Where do the reviews come from?

You import them from a CSV file today. Connecting Google Business Profile to bring in reviews automatically is planned, but it needs Google's approval and is not live yet.

Who runs it?

PraiseLoop is based in Ontario, Canada.

What does it cost?

There is a free plan and a paid plan. Paid pricing will be shown before anyone is asked to pay. Ask about the pilot for the current terms.

What happens to my data?

The privacy policy explains what PraiseLoop collects, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.
